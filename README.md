# UniMate 🧭

**Your university, simplified.**

UniMate is an AI-powered university assistant designed to help students navigate academic, administrative, and campus-related questions through one simple conversational interface.

## 💡 The Problem

University information is often scattered across different offices, websites, documents, notice boards, and student groups. Students—especially freshmen—can struggle to figure out:

* Where an office or facility is located
* Which office handles a specific request
* How to complete university procedures
* Important registration dates and deadlines
* Where to find forms and requirements
* How to navigate campus
* Who to contact when they need help

Sometimes the hardest part isn't getting the answer—it's knowing **who to ask in the first place**.

## 🚀 Our Solution

UniMate brings university information into one accessible platform where students can ask questions naturally and receive clear, actionable answers based on verified university information.

For example:

> "I need to get a transcript. Where do I go?"

Instead of sending students to search through multiple sources, UniMate can provide the relevant office, location, requirements, procedure, and other useful information in one response.

## ✨ Planned Features

* 💬 AI-powered university Q&A
* 🏢 Office and service directory
* 🗺️ Campus navigation
* 📅 Academic deadlines and important dates
* 📋 Step-by-step guides for university procedures
* 🔎 Search across university documents and resources
* 🔗 Source-backed answers to improve reliability

## 🧠 How It Will Work

UniMate will use a retrieval-based AI approach to connect students with university-specific information.

```text
Student Question
       ↓
   AI Assistant
       ↓
University Knowledge Base
       ↓
Relevant Information
       ↓
Clear, Actionable Answer
```

The knowledge base may include university policies, academic calendars, forms, office information, course information, campus maps, and other official resources.

## 🎯 Goal

Our goal is to make university information easier to access and understand, helping students spend less time figuring out **where to go and who to ask**, and more time focusing on their education.
